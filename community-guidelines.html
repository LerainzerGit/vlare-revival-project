Community Guidelines

How we expect people to behave on Vlare.

Vlare is intended to be more creator-friendly than the biggest video platforms, but “less strict” does not mean “no rules.” These guidelines are designed to protect the community while keeping room for commentary, satire, controversial opinions, and unusual content.

1. Be reasonably respectful

You may disagree with other users, criticize people or ideas, and participate in arguments. Do not use Vlare to threaten, stalk, or deliberately intimidate people. Personal attacks that are primarily intended to drive someone off the platform may be removed.

2. No credible threats or calls for violence

Threats of violence, instructions or encouragement to seriously harm someone, and content coordinating real-world violence are not allowed. Lawful discussion of war, violence, crime, history, news, fiction, games, or self-defense is not prohibited just because violence is mentioned.

3. Do not publish private or sensitive personal information

Do not post another person’s private address, phone number, passwords, financial credentials, authentication codes, or other information that could reasonably put them at risk. Public information about public figures and legitimate reporting are not automatically prohibited.

4. No scams, fraud, or impersonation

Do not use Vlare to run scams, phishing operations, fake giveaways, deceptive investment schemes, or fraudulent services. Do not impersonate another person, creator, company, or Vlare staff in a way that is likely to mislead people.

5. Spam and platform abuse

Avoid repetitive unsolicited promotion, mass-created accounts, automated engagement, view manipulation, comment flooding, and attempts to interfere with the operation of Vlare. Normal self-promotion and linking to your own work are allowed when they are relevant and not abusive.

6. Copyright and rights of others

You are responsible for having the rights needed to upload material. Do not knowingly upload material that infringes someone else’s copyright, trademark, privacy, publicity, or other legal rights. Vlare may remove content when required by law or when a valid rights complaint is received.

7. Adult and sensitive material

Do not upload illegal sexual content, sexual exploitation material involving minors, or content depicting or facilitating non-consensual sexual abuse. Vlare may also apply age restrictions, visibility limits, or warnings to lawful material that is sexually explicit, graphically violent, or otherwise unsuitable for younger viewers.

8. Illegal content

Content that directly facilitates serious illegal activity may be removed. Educational, journalistic, documentary, historical, fictional, or critical discussion can be allowed when it does not function as a practical guide to wrongdoing.

9. Malware and security abuse

Do not distribute malware, credential stealers, destructive code, or links intended to compromise another person’s device or account. Security research should be conducted responsibly and without attacking Vlare or other users.

10. Enforcement

Depending on the circumstances, Vlare may warn a user, remove content, restrict features, demonetize content, suspend an account, or terminate an account. Enforcement may take into account context, intent, severity, prior history, and whether a user is repeatedly abusing the platform.

11. Appeals

Users may appeal moderation decisions by contacting [moderation contact email]. Include the account name, affected content, and a short explanation of why you believe the decision should be reconsidered.

12. Changes to these guidelines

Vlare may update these guidelines as the platform develops. The version published on this page is the version that applies at the time of an enforcement decision, subject to any required notice or legal obligations.
